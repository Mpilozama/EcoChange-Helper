How it works

EcoChange turns raw air quality data into decisions: is it a good time to exercise, let kids play outside, or open the windows?

Where the data comes from

Fine dust (PM2.5) readings and forecasts come from Open-Meteo's air quality service. Their values are computed by atmospheric models, not measured by a sensor on your street, so treat them as good estimates, not exact readings.

How the levels work

Levels are simplified bands based on World Health Organization and US EPA guidance. The advice is general and not medical advice. If you have a lung or heart condition, follow your doctor's guidance.

What's coming

A data pipeline that stores readings every day, so the Compare page can show trends and how often each area exceeds the WHO guideline.
