What do these terms mean?

Fine dust (PM2.5) is tiny particles from smoke and exhaust, small enough to get deep into your lungs. It's the main number used to judge air quality.

µg/m³ is just the unit: micrograms of dust in each cubic metre of air.

Ozone is smog near the ground. It can make breathing feel tight, especially on hot days.
